Always-free portable antenna plans for the every-ham. Computer modeled, homebrewed, and field-tested.

 

Designs Subscribe Plans Primers Shop Press Clubs Newsletters Sites Music Heroes Donate About Contact

My mission is to design efficient and effective portable antennas for the every-ham that integrate performance (high radiation efficiency), elegance (speed and simplicity of deployment), and convenience (ease of transport and storage). These designs are always shared for free with the global amateur radio community to encourage more operators to homebrew their own portable antennas.

What's New

In October, I introduced my free PDF, Majestic Non-Resonant Antenna, a non-resonant, no touch antenna for 40M–6M that requires no adjustment of the 25' telescoping whip or the 33' counterpoise to change bands. Explore the design!

In September, I introduced my free PDF, Thruster Triangular Loop, a right-triangular loop antenna for 20M–6M that features bidirectional gain through the loop along with broadband coverage, low noise pickup, and excellent directional performance. Take a look!

In August, I introduced a new revised free PDF for the Marauder Parasitic Array, a Moxon-inspired 2-element gain antenna for 20M–10M. The updated design features more gain, more front-to-back, and increased radiation efficiency. Give it a read!

In July, I introduced my free Primer PDF, Antennas Primer 2, which focuses on Antenna System Efficiency: Effective Radiated Power (ERP) from the signal source through the transmission feedline to the antenna structure. Check it out!
